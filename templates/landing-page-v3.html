What is DropAnywhere?

Your email is the app.

DropAnywhere gives you a private email address — your Vault. Send it anything: a thought, a to-do, a PDF, a voice note, a forwarded flight confirmation.

Behind the scenes, AI reads and organizes everything you send. Then every morning, you get one clean email — your Daily Digest — with your priorities sorted, your ideas captured, and your chaos turned into clarity.

No app to install. No account to manage. No habits to build.
