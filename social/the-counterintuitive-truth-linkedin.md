## The Post

The counterintuitive truth about your best ideas:

They don't want to be organized.

After building DropAnywhere, I've watched 200+ people struggle with the same thing:

They capture a brilliant insight → spend 15 minutes deciding where it belongs → lose the moment completely.

Your brain doesn't think in folders. It thinks in connections.

When you force ideas into rigid structures, you break the natural flow that made them brilliant in the first place.

**What works instead:**

Drop the idea anywhere. Don't categorize. Don't tag. Don't organize.

Tomorrow, AI finds the patterns you missed. Shows you connections you never saw. Surfaces insights when you actually need them.

Not when you think you need them. When you *actually* need them.

I tested this for 6 months. Counter to every productivity system I'd ever used.

Result? More breakthrough moments in the last month than the previous year.

The secret isn't better organization. It's trusting that your brain already knows how ideas fit together.

You just need a system that thinks like you do.

**Sometimes the best way to capture lightning is to stop trying to put it in a bottle.**
