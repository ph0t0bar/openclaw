# LinkedIn Launch Announcement — Founder Story

Three months ago, I was drowning in my own thoughts.

Voice memos piling up. Random ideas scattered across 12 different apps. That familiar feeling of "I know I thought something important yesterday, but where the hell did I write it?"

I tried every productivity system on earth. Notion databases. Obsidian vaults. Voice-to-text apps that promised to "capture everything." But they all had the same flaw: they made me the janitor of my own brain.

Then I realized something: I wasn't looking for another place to store things. I needed something that would think *with* me.

So I built DropAnywhere.

Here's how it works: You drop thoughts anywhere—email, text, voice memo, whatever feels natural in the moment. Then you forget about them. Completely. The system thinks about your drops overnight and sends you one beautiful email digest each morning with the connections your brain couldn't see.

No categorizing. No tagging. No "processing your inbox." Just drop it, forget it, wake up lighter.

I've been dogfooding this for 3 months. Yesterday I dropped a random feeling about being stuck on music. This morning I woke up to a digest that connected that feeling to an old conversation with my partner about creativity. By lunch, I was in the studio. Tonight I'm starting a 30-day songwriting challenge with her.

The system saw something I couldn't. That's when I knew we had something real.

Your brain isn't broken. You're just trying to do a machine's job with human hardware.

DropAnywhere launches this Friday. First 100 people get free lifetime access.

Drop it. Forget it. Wake up lighter.
