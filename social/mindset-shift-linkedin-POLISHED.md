The most successful people I know didn't grind harder.

They stopped fighting themselves.

I used to believe success required suffering. Late nights. Burnout. The "no days off" mentality.

Then I noticed something strange: The people actually building great things weren't exhausted. They were... flowing.

Here's what I learned:

When you're aligned internally, decisions come faster. Ideas connect naturally. Problems solve themselves while you're in the shower.

When you're fighting internal resistance? Every task takes three times longer. Every decision drains you. You're pushing the river uphill.

**The external chaos is usually just a mirror.**

If you're constantly hitting walls, maybe the wall isn't the market. Maybe it's in your head.

I built DropAnywhere from this insight. Instead of forcing my brain into productivity boxes, I gave it space to surprise me.

No categories. No folders. No inbox anxiety.

Just: Drop it. The system finds the patterns. I wake up to insights, not notifications.

Three years of building this taught me: **Flow beats force. Every single time.**
