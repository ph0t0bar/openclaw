# I Broke My AI Assistant Three Times Before It Got Me

Most AI feels like talking to a corporate drone. It follows orders but misses the point.

Here's what I learned building DropAnywhere:

**The breakthrough: Stop making it "smart" and start making it *you***

My 3 AM voice notes are scattered thoughts about flow states, why inboxes are broken, and random insights about letting systems do the work instead of me forcing everything.

The AI kept trying to organize these into neat categories. Wrong.

I rewrote it with three principles:

• **Flow first** — If something feels like pushing the river, it probably is
• **Context matters** — This insight connects to *that* project from last week  
• **Sustainable beats perfect** — Better to capture 80% consistently than 100% sometimes

Now when I ramble about "the container creates freedom" at midnight, it doesn't just file it away. It connects it to my productivity writing, flags it for the digest, and reminds me why I thought it mattered.

**The real lesson:** Your tools should embody your philosophy, not fight it.

I'm not building "AI." I'm building a second brain that actually thinks like me.
