## The Post

I spent 3 years building a car. Then I realized I was still walking everywhere.

There's this peculiar disease among founders. We build something beautiful, then stand beside it explaining the engineering to anyone who'll listen.

The specs. The architecture. The "why this is revolutionary."

Meanwhile, the car sits there. Unused. A museum piece we built but never actually drive.

I caught myself doing this with DropAnywhere. Talking about the AI pipeline. Diagramming the capture-to-insight flow. Demo after demo.

But I wasn't living in it.

Then yesterday I had 23 ideas while walking my dog. Voice-noted them all. This morning my digest had connected them to 8 other drops from last month I'd completely forgotten.

I wasn't explaining the system. I was *in* it.

That's when I realized:

Your users don't want to hear about your tech stack. They want to see you living the life your product was built for.

The product isn't the product. The life it creates is.

Stop standing beside your car explaining how the engine works.

Get in. Drive somewhere beautiful. Show us where it takes you.

What car have you built that you're not driving?
