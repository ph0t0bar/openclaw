## The Post

You've tried them all. So have I.

Notion: Beautiful. Powerful. Requires a PhD in you to maintain.

Obsidian: Incredible for *finding* things. Terrible for *capturing* them at 70mph.

Apple Notes: Graveyard. You know what I mean.

Voice Memos: 47 untranscribed files of you mumbling. Nice.

Evernote: Still exists apparently.

Here's the problem with every single one of them:

They're *storage*.

They hold what you give them. They do nothing while you sleep. They wait for you to be organized — which is exactly the problem.

DropAnywhere is different because it's *active*.

You drop a thought. Email, SMS, voice note, screenshot, whatever.

Overnight, it gets processed, connected to other things you've captured, and shows up in your morning digest.

You don't open a vault. Your insights come to you.

Storage vs. thinking partner.

That's the real difference.
