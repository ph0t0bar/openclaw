# MEGA — The Email Renaissance (LinkedIn Post)

**Type:** Contrarian thought leadership  
**Timing:** Week 2 post-launch (April 1-2, 2026)  
**Theme:** Email > Apps for AI capture  

---

I have a confession: I'm more excited about email than I am about any new app.

Everyone's running from their inbox. Building apps to replace it. But they're missing the point.

Email isn't the problem. Email is the solution.

Here's what I kept noticing: my best ideas don't happen at a desk. They happen in the car. At 2am. Mid-conversation with someone who sparks a thought.

And the fastest way to capture them? Email myself.

Not open an app. Not find the right folder. Just fire off a quick email and forget it.

So we leaned in.

DropAnywhere now treats your inbox like an AI-powered drop zone. Forward a thought. CC us on a conversation. Email yourself a voice note.

Our system reads it, extracts the insight, and weaves it into your daily intelligence digest.

We're calling it MEGA — Make Email Great Again.

Not because email is nostalgic. Because it's frictionless.

Think about it: Email is the only channel that's universal, permanent, and owned by *you*. Every other inbox belongs to a platform. Your email belongs to you.

The best tool isn't the most sophisticated. It's the one you actually use.
