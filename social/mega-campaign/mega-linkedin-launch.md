Your inbox isn't broken.

The 47 apps you downloaded to fix it are.

Let me explain.

At some point, we collectively decided that email — the most universal, reliable, works-on-every-device-ever-made communication protocol in human history — wasn't good enough.

So we downloaded Notion. And Slack. And Todoist. And Obsidian. And Roam. And Trello. And Asana. And Linear. And Monday. And ClickUp. And Coda. And Airtable.

And then we downloaded apps to connect those apps to each other.

And then we watched YouTube videos about how to set up those apps.

And then we downloaded *different* apps because the first ones were "too complex."

Meanwhile, email just sat there. Working. On every device. For every person. With threading. And search. And offline access. And zero onboarding.

Email never needed a rebrand. Email never pivoted. Email never raised a Series B to figure out its business model.

Email just... worked.

So we built DropAnywhere on a radical idea:

**What if email was the app?**

Here's how it works:

→ Have a thought? Email drop@drop-anywhere.com
→ That's it. That's the app.

No login. No workspace. No "getting started" guide. No importing your data from 6 other tools. No monthly fee for the features you actually need.

Your email becomes your capture tool. We handle the rest — organizing, surfacing insights, delivering a daily digest of everything you dropped.

We're calling it MEGA.

**Make Email Great Again.** 🦜

Not because email stopped being great. Because we stopped noticing.

Your inbox called. It wants its job back.

→ Drop your first thought: drop@drop-anywhere.com
→ Or just forward this post to yourself. See? You already know how email works.
