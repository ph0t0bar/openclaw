# MEGA — Make Email Great Again

## Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name:** MEGA — Make Email Great Again
**Tagline:** "Your inbox isn't broken. The 47 apps you downloaded to fix it are."
**Tone:** Satirical but warm. Self-aware. Anti-productivity-industrial-complex. NOT political — this is purely about email being the GOAT that got disrespected.

---

## The Insight

Everyone hates their inbox. But here's the thing — they don't hate *email*. They hate what happened to email.

Email is perfect:
- It's everywhere. Every device, every platform, every person on earth.
- It works offline.
- It's searchable.
- It threads.
- It's free.
- It doesn't require downloading anything.
- It doesn't have a "workspace" you need to "set up."
- Nobody ever said "let me check if you're on email."

But then we buried it. Under Notion and Slack and Todoist and Obsidian and Roam and Trello and Asana and Linear and Monday and ClickUp and Coda and Airtable and 34 others. We downloaded 47 apps to replace the one thing that already worked.

**DropAnywhere makes email your superpower again.** Send a thought to drop@drop-anywhere.com. That's it. No app. No login. No workspace. No onboarding flow. Just email doing what email was always supposed to do.

## Visual Direction

- **The Cap:** Red baseball caps — but with a parrot 🦜 embroidered on the front instead of text. "MEGA" in bold block letters across the back.
- **Primary Colors:** Campaign Red (#C41E3A) + Brooke Cream (#FAF3E8)
- **Secondary:** Sage (#A8B5A0), Copper accents (#B87333)
- **Typography:** Bold, blocky sans-serif for "MEGA." Newsreader for body copy.
- **Imagery:** Nostalgic email iconography. The @ symbol as hero element. Screenshots of beautiful, clean inboxes. Zero app icons.
- **Mood:** Retro-futurist. "What if we went back to the thing that worked?" Energy of a comeback story, not a political movement.

---

## Key Messages

1. **Email is the OG.** Everything else is a remix.
2. **You don't need another app.** You need fewer apps.
3. **Drop it. Forget it. Wake up lighter.** The DropAnywhere promise.
4. **Your inbox is the last open platform.** No algorithm. No feed. No engagement metrics. Just you and your thoughts.
5. **MEGA isn't a movement. It's a return.** Back to the tool that never needed a rebrand.

## Audience

- Founders and indie hackers drowning in tools
- Knowledge workers with "productivity stack guilt"
- Anyone who's ever set up a Notion template and never looked at it again
- People who secretly just email themselves notes already

## Anti-Targets

- Productivity influencers selling courses on "building a second brain"
- Anyone who unironically uses the phrase "knowledge management system"
- People who think the answer to too many apps is one more app (wait...)
