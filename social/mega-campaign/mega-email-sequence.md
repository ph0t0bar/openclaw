- **CTA buttons:** Copper (#B87333) with cream text, rounded corners
- **Header:** MEGA wordmark in Campaign Red (#C41E3A)
- **Footer:** Parrot emoji + "Make Email Great Again" + unsubscribe link
- **Width:** 600px max, mobile-responsive
