## The Post

You've tried them all. So have I.

**Notion:** Beautiful. Powerful. Requires a PhD in yourself to maintain.

**Obsidian:** Incredible for finding things. Terrible for capturing them while you're driving.

**Apple Notes:** Digital graveyard. You know exactly what I mean.

**Voice Memos:** 47 untranscribed files of you mumbling in your car.

**Evernote:** Still exists, apparently.

Here's the real problem with every single one:

They're storage containers.

They sit there. Hold what you give them. Wait for you to remember to organize. Which is exactly why your best ideas vanish.

DropAnywhere is different because it thinks while you sleep.

You drop a thought — email, SMS, voice note, screenshot, whatever. Overnight, it processes everything, finds connections, and delivers insights to your morning digest.

You don't dig through folders. Your brain comes to you.

Storage vs. thinking partner.

That's not an upgrade. That's a different game.
