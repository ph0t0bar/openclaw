## The Post

I deleted my to-do list app yesterday.

All 247 tasks. Gone.

Here's what hit me: **Inboxes train you to process, not think.**

Email inbox → clear it  
Task inbox → check it off  
Notes inbox → file it away  

But your best ideas don't arrive organized.

They show up at 2 AM. In the shower. While you're stuck in traffic.

The second you shove them into a folder, you've killed what made them worth having.

**Your brain doesn't have an inbox.** It has connections. Random firings. Weird links between things that shouldn't go together.

What if your tools worked the same way?

No filing decisions. No folder hierarchies. No guilt about that stack of "unprocessed" items.

Just drop the thought and move on.

Wake up tomorrow and find it connected to something you thought last month.

That's how we built DropAnywhere.

No folders to choose. No categories to remember. No "inbox zero" anxiety.

Just capture anything, anywhere. Let the AI find the patterns while you sleep.

Launch week has been insane. Same feedback over and over:

**"It's like my brain worked overnight."**

Exactly.
