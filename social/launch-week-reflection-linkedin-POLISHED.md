## The Post

Day 7 of launch week. Real talk time.

What I thought would happen: Perfect demos. Smooth onboarding. Users instantly "getting it."

What actually happened: Our best users broke our assumptions in the best possible way.

Three things that changed everything:

**1. People don't want to organize thoughts. They want thoughts to organize themselves.**

We built beautiful categories and tags. Users ignored them completely. 

Instead, they just... dropped stuff. Random voice notes. Screenshot dumps. Half-formed ideas at 2 AM.

And our AI started connecting dots we never saw coming.

**2. The magic happens at 7 AM, not 7 PM.**

We obsessed over the input experience. Turns out, the real value is waking up to insights you didn't know you had. 

One user: "It's like my brain worked overnight while I slept."

**3. Email beats every fancy app we built.**

Our most engaged users? They email themselves thoughts and CC our system. 

No apps to download. No new habits to form. Just working with how their brain already works.

Launch week taught me that users don't want what you built. They want what you built to *become*.

The difference is everything.

This week: We're rebuilding onboarding around what people actually do, not what we think they should do.

Building in public is humbling. But it's the only way to build something real.
