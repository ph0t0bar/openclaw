## Final Post

I'm terrible at remembering things.

Always have been. Ask my girlfriend.

But here's the thing — trying to get better at remembering is rowing upstream. My brain wasn't built to be a filing cabinet. It was built to connect dots.

Every productivity tool I've tried wants me to become a librarian. Sort this. Tag that. Build folders within folders until I have a beautiful, organized mess that I never look at again.

Screw that.

I don't need a better memory. I need something that remembers FOR me so I can focus on what I'm actually good at — thinking.

That's why I built DropAnywhere.

Email random thoughts. Voice note something overheard. Screenshot whatever catches your eye. Drop it. Forget it completely.

Tomorrow morning? Everything's connected. Patterns emerge. That thing you forgot about suddenly makes sense next to this new thing you just captured.

No filing. No organizing. No guilt about the mess.

Just capture and let the system do what systems do best — see what you can't see when you're living in it.

Your second brain has no inbox because inboxes are where thoughts go to die.
