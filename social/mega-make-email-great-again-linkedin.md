# MEGA — Make Email Great Again

We spent $47 billion on productivity apps last year.

Our productivity went down.

Here's what nobody's saying: Your inbox isn't broken. The 47 apps you downloaded to fix it are.

---

I built 12 different note-taking systems trying to solve one problem.

Every system was beautiful. Every system failed.

Not because the apps were bad. Because I had to *become a different person* to use them.

Notion requires you to be organized. Obsidian requires you to be consistent. Todoist requires you to be on top of your shit.

I'm none of those things. My best ideas come at 2am, in the shower, while I'm driving and can't write anything down.

---

So we're launching DropAnywhere with no app.

No dashboard.
No login.
No organizing.

Just email.

Forward anything to drop@drop-anywhere.com. Get your insights tomorrow morning.

That's it.

---

Email is the only universal protocol everyone already has. It works on every device. It integrates with everything. You don't need to download anything or learn a new interface.

You already know how to email yourself. We just made it useful.

---

MEGA isn't a political statement. It's a product philosophy.

Make Email Great Again means returning to simplicity. Removing instead of adding. Building for how people actually work, not how productivity gurus say they should.

Sometimes the bravest product decision is deleting features.

---

DropAnywhere launches March 24.

No app. Just email. Better than 47 productivity tools combined.
