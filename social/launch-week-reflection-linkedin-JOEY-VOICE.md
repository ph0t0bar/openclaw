## The Post

Day 7 of launch week. Time to be honest.

What I thought would happen: Users would love our beautiful categories and tags. They'd organize their thoughts like productivity gurus.

What actually happened: They completely ignored everything we built. And that taught us everything.

**People don't want to organize thoughts. They want thoughts to organize themselves.**

Our most active users? They just dump stuff. Voice notes while walking the dog. Screenshot chaos at 2 AM. Random ideas that make no sense.

Then our AI starts connecting dots nobody saw coming.

One user told me: "It's like my brain worked overnight while I slept."

That's when it clicked.

**The magic isn't the input. It's the output.**

We obsessed over making capture perfect. Turns out, the real value is waking up to insights you didn't know you had.

**Email beats every fancy app.**

Want to know our most engaged users? They email themselves thoughts and CC our system. 

No apps to download. No new workflow to learn. Just working with how their brain already works.

Launch week broke all our assumptions. In the best way.

Users don't want what you built. They want what you built to become.

We're rebuilding onboarding around what people actually do, not what we think they should do.

Building in public is humbling. But it's the only way to build something that matters.
