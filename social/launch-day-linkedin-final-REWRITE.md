## Rewritten Copy (Joey Voice)

DropAnywhere is live.

I built this because I kept losing good ideas to bad systems.

Picture this: 2am breakthrough about user onboarding. I grab my phone, open Notion. Stare at 47 empty templates trying to figure out where one sentence belongs. 

By the time I picked a template, I'd forgotten the idea.

That's when it hit me. The problem wasn't my brain. It was everything between thinking something and keeping it.

So I built the opposite:

Email yourself a random thought? Tomorrow's digest will have it connected to three other things you forgot you thought about.

Voice note while driving? Transcribed and waiting when you get home.

Screenshot something interesting? Already analyzed and linked to your other screenshots.

2am breakthrough? Captured. Connected. Ready when you wake up.

No organizing. No folders. No decisions about where things "belong."

I've been using it for months. My beta testers averaged over 4 drops a day. Best feedback: "I finally stopped losing ideas."

Your brain is working fine. Your tools just suck.

drop-anywhere.com
