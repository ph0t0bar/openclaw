## The Post

Three years ago I lost a business idea in the shower.

I know how that sounds.

But I genuinely convinced myself: I'll remember this. I don't need to write it down. This one is different.

I did not remember it.

I rebuilt 12 note-taking systems trying to solve it. Notion, Obsidian, paper notebooks, voice memos, a whiteboard in the bathroom (seriously).

Each worked for about two weeks.

Then life happened. The system required effort I didn't have. Ideas came at inconvenient times. The folder structure that made sense in March made no sense by May.

I kept losing things.

The real problem wasn't my apps. It was me trying to organize my brain from the outside.

Your brain doesn't work in folders. It works in connections, associations, random sparks at 2am.

So I stopped trying to build a system.

I built DropAnywhere instead.

Send it anywhere. Get your insights tomorrow.

No organizing. No perfect system. No pretending your brain works in folders.

Just thinking—and a morning digest that does the connecting for you.

It took losing that shower idea to build something I actually use.

Hopefully you won't have to wait as long.
