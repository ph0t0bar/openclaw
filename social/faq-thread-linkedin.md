**Post 1 (Hook):**

---

**Post 2:**

> Q: Why not just use Apple Notes?
>
> A: You ever find a note from 6 months ago and go "holy shit I forgot I thought about this"?
>
> That's the problem. Apple Notes stores. It doesn't connect. It doesn't remind you.
>
> DropAnywhere finds patterns across your drops and surfaces them when relevant.
> Storage vs. thinking partner.

---

**Post 3:**

> Q: What if I drop something sensitive?
>
> A: Same encryption as your bank. We can't read your drops even if we wanted to.
>
> (And we don't want to. Your data is expensive headache we don't need.)

---

**Post 4:**

> Q: How is this different from AI chatbots?
>
> A: Chatbots wait for you to ask.
>
> DropAnywhere works while you sleep. You drop raw thoughts. It organizes, connects, and delivers insights to your inbox every morning.
>
> Push vs. pull.

---

**Post 5:**

> Q: Do I have to change how I work?
>
> A: God no. That's the whole point.
>
> Drop thoughts anywhere — email, voice, text. Keep using whatever you use.
>
> We just make sure you actually see them again.

---
