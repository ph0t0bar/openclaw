I deleted my productivity app's inbox yesterday.

All 247 unread items. Gone.

So I built something different.

No managing. No organizing. No guilt.

Just: Here's what your brain captured. Here's what it means. Now go create.

I used to spend mornings digging through notifications. Now I wake up to insights.

**Drop it. Forget it. Wake up lighter.**

That's the whole philosophy.

Your brain isn't a filing cabinet. It's a launching pad.
