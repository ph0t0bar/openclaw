## Suggested Improvements

### 1. Formatting Consistency
The bullets use both `→` and `•`. Pick one style (recommend `→` for the transformation feel).

### 2. Add Micro-Social Proof (if available)
Even one line helps:
> "Beta testers averaged 4.2 drops/day. 89% said they 'stopped losing ideas.'"

If no hard numbers, use qualitative:
> "The most common feedback from beta testers: 'I stopped losing ideas.'"

### 3. Visual Specification
Current: "Clean product screenshot"
Better: "GIF: Email with subject 'podcast idea about AI' → next morning showing digest with 'AI podcast idea' + 2 related drops auto-linked"

### 4. Hook Variation for Testing
Consider A/B testing this alternative opening:
> "I lost a $50K idea to a blank Notion page."

It's punchier and creates urgency. The "47 templates" detail can come later.

### 5. Hashtag Strategy
Current: #productivity #ai #secondbrain #dropanywhere #launch

Consider adding:
- #founder (for founder audience)
- #buildinpublic (for indie hacker reach)
- Replace #ai with #artificialintelligence (broader LinkedIn algorithm pickup)
