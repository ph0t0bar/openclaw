# Stop Rowing Upstream — LinkedIn Post
*Joey's Voice — March 17, 2026*

---

**I used to think grinding harder was the answer. I was wrong.**

Three years ago: 14-hour days, checking metrics every 20 minutes, burning through coffee like it was my job. "If it doesn't hurt, I'm not doing it right."

The product flopped. I burned out so hard I couldn't look at code for two months.

Fast forward: DropAnywhere took half the time with 1/3 the stress. 

What changed? I stopped fighting the river.

**Here's what I mean:**

When you're forcing something, everything becomes twice as hard. Features feel clunky. Marketing sounds desperate. Even the bugs seem personal.

But when you're swimming with the current? Code flows. The right people show up. Ideas hit you in the shower and actually work.

**My test now:** "Does this make me feel heavier or lighter?"

Heavy = obligation, "should," someone else's playbook  
Light = excitement, curiosity, my gut saying "hell yes"

I follow the light. Every single time.

**Real example:** I spent months building a complex workflow system because that's what "serious" productivity tools do. Pure misery. Like pushing a boulder uphill in quicksand.

Then one day: "What if people could just email themselves thoughts and get them back organized the next morning?"

That became DropAnywhere's core. Built in two weeks. Users actually use it.

The difference? I stopped rowing upstream and let the current carry me.

**Your work doesn't have to feel like a fight.**

Find the thing that seems obvious to you but impossible to everyone else. That's your river.

Drop the oars.
