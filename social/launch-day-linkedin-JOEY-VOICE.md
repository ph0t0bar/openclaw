# Launch Day LinkedIn — Joey's Voice

DropAnywhere is live.

I built this because productivity apps broke my brain.

Last Thursday, 2am. Had this perfect idea about why our users bounce after signup. Crystal clear. The kind of insight that makes you sit up in bed.

Opened Notion to write one sentence.

Stared at 47 empty templates for 10 minutes trying to figure out which folder a single thought belonged in.

Forgot the entire idea while deciding between "Quick Captures" and "Random Thoughts."

That's when I realized: we didn't build tools to help us think. We built anxiety machines that make us organize our way out of having ideas.

So I built the opposite.

Email yourself a random thought → tomorrow's digest finds the patterns
Voice note while driving → transcribed, connected to your other ideas  
Screenshot something interesting → analyzed, linked to relevant drops
2am breakthrough → captured, waiting when you wake up

No folders. No templates. No losing good ideas to bad systems.

Just: drop it anywhere, forget about it, wake up with insights.

Your brain works. Your tools should get out of the way.

→ drop-anywhere.com
