# Brand Guide — Public Identity

## Names
- **Product:** DropAnywhere (or "Drop" for short)
- **Company:** DropAnywhere
- **Mascot:** The Parrot 🦜
- **Tagline:** "Drop it. Forget it. Wake up lighter."

## Public-Facing Rules
- NEVER use "Claw", "Clawd", or "OpenClaw" in any customer/public communication
- "Claw" is internal infrastructure only — users never see it
- The AI assistant is called "Drop" externally
- The parrot 🦜 is the symbol/mascot — use it in footers, signatures, social
- "Drop" is both a verb (drop your thoughts) and the assistant's name

## Voice in Emails
- Sign off as "Drop" or use the parrot 🦜
- "Reply to this email — Drop reads every reply. 🦜"
- Subject prefixes use emoji, not names

## Examples
- ✅ "Drop here — your one-pager is attached"
- ✅ "🦜 Got it — processing now"
- ✅ "The parrot's on it"
- ❌ "Claw here"
- ❌ "OpenClaw assistant"
- ❌ "Your Claw AI"
