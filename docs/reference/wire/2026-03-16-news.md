### 17:28 UTC — AI Productivity Paradox Report
**Source:** ActivTrak Productivity Lab 2026 State of the Workplace
**Tag:** 🟡 relevant

**Key Findings:**
- **80% of employees now use AI tools** (up 52% from 2 years ago)
- **Productivity paradox:** AI is accelerating work, not reducing it
  - Email time: +104%
  - Chat/messaging: +145%
  - Business management tools: +94%
  - Collaboration surged 34%
  - **Focus time hit 3-year low** (-23 min/day for AI users)
- Companies now use **7+ AI tools on average** (vs 2 in 2023)
- Optimal AI usage: **7-10% of work hours** = 95% productivity
  - Only **3% of users** hit this sweet spot

**"AI Brain Fry" (BCG Study):**
- Workers using 3 or fewer AI tools → productivity gains
- Workers using 4+ tools → cognitive overload, productivity drops

**Economic Impact:**
- Fed St. Louis: AI increases aggregate productivity 1.1% (33% per hour of use)
- Goldman Sachs: No economy-wide productivity boost yet (limited to customer service + dev)
