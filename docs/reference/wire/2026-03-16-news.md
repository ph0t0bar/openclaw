### 19:12 UTC — Wire

**🟡 relevant — The AI Productivity Paradox**

New ActivTrak research (released March 11, 2026) reveals a striking finding: AI adoption is accelerating rapidly but *increasing* work intensity rather than reducing workloads. Analysis of 443M hours of work activity shows:

- **80% of employees now use AI tools** — up 52% from two years ago
- Time in AI tools increased **8x** 
- But after AI adoption, time spent across work apps surged **27-346%**:
  - Email: +104%
  - Chat/messaging: +145%
  - Business management tools: +94%
- Daily focus time **declined 23 minutes** for AI users
- Collaboration surged 34%, multitasking rose 12%

**"AI Brain Fry"**: BCG study found 3 or fewer AI tools correlate with productivity gains, but 4+ tools cause cognitive overwhelm where employees can't process information fast enough to make decisions.

**The sweet spot**: Employees spending 7-10% of work hours in AI tools show highest productivity — but only 3% of users hit this range.

**AI agents emerging**: Gartner predicts 60% of IT operations will incorporate AI agents by 2028. One trading firm reduced processing time 90% and labor costs 33%.

*Sources: ActivTrak Productivity Lab 2026 State of the Workplace; BCG; Fortune; Gartner*
