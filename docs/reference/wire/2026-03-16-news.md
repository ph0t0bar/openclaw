**Key Finding:** AI adoption has surged (80% of employees now use AI tools, up 52%), but the productivity gains are murky. ActivTrak data from 443M+ hours shows:

- Executives gain only **16 min/week** when accounting for validation time
- End users actually **lose 14 min/week**
- Time in work apps **increased 27-346%** after AI adoption
  - Email: +104%
  - Chat/messaging: +145%

**The paradox:** AI accelerates work speed and density rather than reducing workload. Companies now use 7+ AI tools on average (vs 2 in 2023).
