## 2026-03-16 12:24 UTC — AI Productivity Tools News

🟡 **RELEVANT** — March 2026 AI productivity landscape shifting from "replacement" to "acceleration"

### Key Finding: AI Is Accelerating Work, Not Replacing It

**ActivTrak's March 11 report** (443M hours analyzed across 1,111 orgs):
- Companies now average **7+ AI tools** (up from 2 in 2023)
- ChatGPT dominates the stack
- **Post-adoption work activity surged:**
  - Email: +104%
  - Chat: +145%
  - Focus time: **-23 minutes/day**
  - Multitasking: +12%
  - Workday: actually 2% *shorter*

**The "AI Brain Fry" Problem** (BCG survey of 1,488 workers):
- Productivity rises with ≤3 tools
- **Plummets beyond 4 tools** — cognitive overload kicks in
- 80% of employees now using AI tools (8x usage time increase)

**The Sweet Spot:**
- Optimal use: **7-10% of hours** → 95% productivity gain
- Reality: Only **3% of users** hit this benchmark

### What This Means for DropAnywhere
The inbox-less philosophy aligns with the anti-overload trend. Most users drown in 7+ tools. A unified capture + digest system (what we're building) directly addresses the "too many tools" problem BCG identified.
