# Virtual Styling Assistant: Crawl-Walk-Run Launch Plan
**Project:** Ari Chesterman Virtual Styling Assistant  
**Concept:** AI-powered personal styling subscription service  
**Date:** March 15, 2026  
**Prepared for:** Ari Chesterman

---

## Executive Summary

Transform Ari's natural styling expertise into a scalable AI-powered subscription service. The phased approach captures Ari's styling philosophy, trains AI on real conversations, and gradually transitions from human-led to AI-assisted delivery while maintaining Ari's voice and quality.

**Revenue Model:** Monthly subscription ($49-149/month tiers)  
**Target Market:** Professional women 25-45 seeking personal styling without luxury price tags

## Phase 1: CRAWL — 3 Months (90 Days)

### Objective: Capture Ari's Philosophy & Train the AI

**Time Investment:** 5-10 hours/week  
**Start → End:** Month 0 → Month 3  
**Milestone:** First AI-assisted response sent

---

**Weeks 1-2: Documentation (10-20 hours total)**
- [ ] Record 10-20 voice memos explaining styling philosophy
  - Core principles (fit, color, occasion, confidence)
  - "Dressier vs. casual" framework
  - Wardrobe essentials philosophy
  - Shopping decision criteria
  - Body type considerations
  - Seasonal transition strategies
  
- [ ] Document 20-30 real styling conversations (like the Chesterman example)
  - Export from iMessage/WhatsApp
  - Anonymize client names
  - Tag with: occasion, client type, season, key recommendations
  
- [ ] Create "Ari's Styling DNA" document
  - Brand voice guidelines (casual, encouraging, direct)
  - Do's and don'ts
  - Signature phrases and approaches
  - Common recommendations (white pants, blazer importance, etc.)

**Week 3-4: Content Foundation**
- [ ] Build recommendation library
  - 50+ outfit formulas with photos
  - Shopping links to preferred retailers
  - "Ari-approved" item database
  - Seasonal lookbooks
  
- [ ] Create client onboarding flow
  - Style quiz (lifestyle, preferences, budget, body type)
  - Wardrobe audit guide
  - Goal-setting conversation template

**Week 5-8: Tech Setup (Minimum Viable)**

| Component | Tool | Purpose | Cost |
|-----------|------|---------|------|
| **AI Backend** | OpenRouter (Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Kimi K2.5) | Core styling intelligence | ~$50-150/mo |
| **Chat Interface** | Client's preferred texting app | Communication (Ari already texts clients) | Free |
| **Photo Storage** | Simple folder system | Outfit photos, client uploads | Free |
| **CRM/Database** | Spreadsheet | Client profiles, preferences, history | Free |
| **Scheduling** | Calendar tool | Consultation bookings | Free tier |
| **Payment** | Stripe | Subscription billing | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| **In-person** | Shopping trips | Physical styling sessions | — |

**Week 9-12: Beta Pilot**
- [ ] Recruit 10 beta clients (friends, referrals)
- [ ] Manual styling with template assistance
- [ ] Document every interaction for training data
- [ ] Gather feedback on response style, recommendations, timing

**Crawl Phase Deliverables:**
- ✅ Styling philosophy documented

## Phase 2: WALK — 3 Months (Months 4-6)

### Objective: AI Learns, Ari Reviews, Revenue Starts

**Time Investment:** 10-15 hours/week  
**Start → End:** Month 3 → Month 6  
**Milestone:** First paying subscriber

---

**Month 4: AI Training (Weeks 13-16)**
**Time:** 15-20 hours total (intensive setup)

1. **Fine-tuning Process**
   - Export all documented conversations (JSON format)
   - Structure training data with client context, conversation flow, recommendations
   - Include outcomes (purchases made, confidence increases)

2. **AI Persona Development (via OpenRouter)**
   - Models per OpenRouter leaderboard (Mar 2026): Claude Sonnet 4.6 (#5), Gemini 2.5 Flash (#8), Kimi K2.5 (#9)
   - Route: Claude Sonnet 4.6 for complex styling, Gemini 2.5 Flash for quick responses, Kimi K2.5 for fallback
   - System prompt incorporating Ari's voice
   - Temperature tuning (0.7 for creativity, consistency checks)
   - Multi-turn conversation handling
   - Photo analysis integration (Claude Sonnet 4.6 Vision)

3. **Quality Control Framework**
   - AI generates recommendation
   - Ari reviews before sending (initially 100%, then 50%)
   - Feedback loop: Ari edits → AI learns
   - Confidence scoring: AI flags uncertain recommendations

**Month 5: The Pass-Off Protocol (Weeks 17-20)**
**Timeline:** 4 weeks to full AI autonomy
**Time:** 10-12 hours/week (decreasing each week)

| Week | AI Role | Ari Role | Time Required | Client Sees |
|------|---------|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Week 17 | Drafts responses | Edits 100% | 12 hrs/week | "Ari suggested..." |
| Week 18 | Drafts, suggests | Reviews 75% | 10 hrs/week | "Ari suggested..." |
| Week 19 | Drafts, learns patterns | Spot-checks 50% | 8 hrs/week | "Ari suggested..." |
| Week 20 | Responds independently | Monitors, intervenes | 5 hrs/week | "Ari suggested..." |

**Pass-Off Triggers:**
- High-confidence scenarios (outfit feedback, basic recommendations)
- Repeat client patterns (Ari has styled them 5+ times)
- Common occasions (work, weekend, dinner)

**Human Escalation Triggers:**
- Special events (weddings, galas, job interviews)
- Body image concerns or emotional styling needs
- Complex wardrobe overhauls
- High-value shopping decisions ($500+)

**Month 6: Subscription Launch (Weeks 21-24)**
**Timeline:** First paying clients onboarded  
**Time:** 10-15 hours/week (managing subscriptions + ongoing styling)

| Tier | Price | Includes | AI/Human Mix |
|------|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Essential** | $49/mo | 5 styling requests, outfit feedback, shopping links | 90% AI, 10% Ari |
| **Signature** | $99/mo | Unlimited requests, priority response, seasonal planning | 70% AI, 30% Ari |
| **Concierge** | $149/mo | Everything + monthly 1:1 video call, personal shopping | 50% AI, 50% Ari |

**Walk Phase Deliverables:**
- ✅ Fine-tuned AI model trained on Ari's conversations
- ✅ Pass-off protocol operational
- ✅ 50 paying subscribers across tiers
- ✅ Quality assurance system running

**Revenue Target:** $3,000-5,000/month

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **AI sounds robotic** | Extensive fine-tuning, Ari edits all outputs initially |
| **Client privacy concerns** | Clear data policy, opt-in AI training, anonymized data |
| **Quality drops with scale** | Strict QA, escalation triggers, client satisfaction surveys |
| **Ari burns out during transition** | Gradual pass-off, revenue justifies effort |
| **Competition** | Ari's unique voice, established reputation, personalization depth |
