# 🧢 The SnapBack Offer

> **"Bring me one problem. Talk about it for a week. I'll show you what you're not seeing."**

## How It Works

1. **Sign up with one issue** — career stuck, relationship loop, business decision, health goal, creative block
2. **7 days of email prompts** — just reply. Text, screenshots, voice notes, whatever. No app. No platform. Just your inbox.
3. **Day 8: Your SnapBack arrives** — your own words, reflected back as the narrative you couldn't see from inside. The pattern. The thread. The answer that was already in your drops.

## Why It Works

The free week IS the demo. You don't explain the vault — they FEEL it when their scattered week comes back as a story.
